Our workshop enforces uncompromising hand-crafting protocols, utilizing single-estate natural materials, precision micro-instrumentation, and extensive individual bench testing exceeding international industry standards.

Depending on material procurement and hand-finishing complexity, bespoke commissions require between eight and sixteen weeks of dedicated artisan labor. Patrons receive periodic milestone documentation throughout production.

Every piece includes a comprehensive twenty-four-month warranty covering structural integrity and craftsmanship defects under normal metropolitan care, backed by our dedicated Manhattan service atelier.
